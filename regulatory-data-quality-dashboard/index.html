Power BI case study

Regulatory Data Quality Dashboard

An interactive Power BI reporting suite bringing data quality exceptions, regulatory impact and severity together so teams can see where exposure is concentrated, understand what is driving it and prioritise remediation activity.

The brief

Move beyond counting issues to understanding where they matter most.

Organisations may know that data quality issues exist, but the number of exceptions alone does not necessarily show where the greatest regulatory or operational concern lies.

Different issues can affect different obligations, processes and customer groups, while some exceptions may carry considerably greater significance than others.

This Power BI model brings data quality exceptions, rules, regulatory mappings, impact and severity information together in one structured reporting suite.

The result is a prioritised view of where attention may be needed, helping governance and operational teams move from a long list of issues to a clearer understanding of relative exposure and remediation priorities.

What decision-makers can see

From individual exceptions to a prioritised view of risk.

Regulatory readiness

A consolidated indicator provides a view of the current data quality position against the mapped regulatory requirements, taking account of the issues identified and their relative impact.

Relative risk exposure

Exceptions are considered alongside regulatory impact and severity rather than being treated equally. This helps distinguish high-volume issues from those that may warrant more immediate attention.

Remediation priorities

Users can identify the rules, obligations and data quality issues contributing most to the overall position and use that information to support prioritisation of remediation activity.

Business questions

Designed around the questions governance teams need to answer.

01

Where is exposure concentrated?

Identify which regulatory obligations, rules or parts of the organisation are most affected by current data quality issues.

02

Which issues matter most?

Consider exception volume alongside impact and severity so prioritisation is not based on counts alone.

03

Where should remediation begin?

Focus attention on the issues contributing most to the overall position and where improvement may have the greatest effect.

04

Is the position improving?

Track readiness and risk indicators over time as issues are addressed and data quality improves.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured regulatory data model

Data quality exceptions, rules, regulatory obligations, severity and impact information are connected through a structured Power BI analytical model.

Risk scoring framework

A weighted approach combines exception information, severity and mapped impact to create a relative view of where exposure is concentrated.

Interactive drill-down

Users can move from executive-level indicators into individual obligations, rules and data quality issues without creating separate reports.

Management reporting

KPI indicators, prioritised views and visual cues are designed to support governance, risk, data quality and remediation discussions.
