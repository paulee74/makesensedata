Power BI case study

Regulatory Data Quality Dashboard

Transforming thousands of data quality exceptions into a clear view of regulatory exposure, readiness and remediation priorities.

The challenge

Move beyond counting data quality issues to understanding regulatory impact.

Organisations often know they have data quality issues, but the scale of those issues does not always reveal where the greatest regulatory exposure exists.

A high volume of exceptions does not necessarily mean the highest risk. Different issues affect different regulatory obligations and require different levels of attention.

This dashboard demonstrates how data quality information can be combined with regulatory impact and severity weighting to identify where remediation effort should be focused.

Live dashboard

Explore the Regulatory Impact Analysis dashboard.

Use the filters to explore regulatory obligations, client classifications, entity types and booking centres. The dashboard highlights regulatory readiness, risk exposure and the data quality issues creating the greatest impact.

Demonstration dashboard using synthetic data. No real customer, employee or regulatory information is shown.

What it shows

A risk-based view of regulatory data quality.

Regulatory Readiness Score

A percentage-based measure showing overall readiness against regulatory requirements by considering identified data quality risks and their impact.

Regulatory Risk Score

Risk scoring goes beyond simply counting exceptions by weighting issues based on regulatory impact and severity. This highlights where unresolved problems create the greatest exposure.

Remediation Priorities

The dashboard identifies the regulations, rules and data quality issues where improvement activity can have the greatest reduction in risk.

Business questions

Designed around the decisions governance teams need to make.

01

Where is regulatory exposure highest?

Identify which regulatory obligations are most affected by current data quality issues.

02

Which issues matter most?

Prioritise exceptions using risk weighting rather than exception volume alone.

03

Where should remediation begin?

Focus improvement activity on the regulations and rules creating the greatest exposure.

04

How is readiness changing?

Track regulatory readiness and risk reduction as data quality improves.

How it was built

The key components.

Structured data model

A Power BI model linking data quality exceptions, rules, regulations, severity and regulatory impact.

Risk scoring framework

A weighted scoring approach combining exception volumes, regulatory impact and severity to create a prioritised view of exposure.

Interactive analysis

Users can move from executive-level readiness indicators into individual rules, regulations and remediation opportunities.

Executive reporting

Clear KPI indicators and visual analysis designed to support governance, risk and data quality discussions.
