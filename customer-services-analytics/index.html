Power BI case study

Customer Service & Contact Centre Analytics

An interactive operational reporting suite bringing contact demand, service performance, customer outcomes and advisor activity together in one clear view.

The brief

Turn operational data into something managers can act on.

Contact centres generate large volumes of operational information, but individual measures do not always reveal where service pressure is occurring or what is affecting the customer experience.

This Power BI model combines contact, channel, team, advisor and customer outcome data in a structured reporting model.

The report provides an executive view of demand, responsiveness, resolution and satisfaction, alongside deeper analysis of teams, channels and individual advisor performance.

Dynamic commentary interprets the current selection in plain language, helping managers move from monitoring performance to identifying where attention is required.

What it shows

Two connected views of service performance.

Executive Operations

A high-level view of contact volume, answer rate, speed of answer, abandonment, handling time, first contact resolution and customer satisfaction.

A demand heatmap highlights busy days and times, while dynamic commentary summarises the key operational and customer experience findings.

Operations Performance

A management view focused on advisor workload, team performance, service-level achievement, handling time and customer outcomes.

The advisor league table uses conditional formatting and data bars to make workload, efficiency, resolution and satisfaction easier to compare.

How it was built

The key pieces.

01

Operational data model

Contact, date, team, advisor, channel and contact-reason data connected through a structured analytical model.

02

Service measures

Contact volumes, answer rate, abandonment, response time, handling time, resolution and satisfaction calculated dynamically.

03

Dynamic commentary

Narrative measures respond to filters and explain performance, demand patterns, team comparisons and improvement priorities.

04

Management analysis

Heatmaps, team comparisons and an advisor league table help managers identify workload pressure and coaching opportunities.

Business questions

Designed around the decisions service managers need to make.

The report helps users answer questions such as:

  • When does contact demand peak?
  • Which channels generate the highest workload?
  • Are customers receiving a timely response?
  • Which teams have the strongest first contact resolution?
  • Is operational performance translating into customer satisfaction?
  • Which advisors may need additional support or coaching?
  • Which contact reasons are driving the most demand?
