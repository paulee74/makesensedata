Power BI case study

Retail & Hospitality Analytics

An interactive Power BI reporting suite bringing sales, margins, labour, waste and targets together so managers can compare locations, understand what is driving performance and see where attention is needed.

The brief

Sales alone do not tell the whole story.

A busy location can generate strong revenue while still performing less efficiently because of higher labour costs, lower margins or greater waste.

Equally, a smaller location may generate less revenue but operate efficiently and make a strong contribution to the business.

This Power BI model brings sales, transaction, product, staffing, target and waste information together in one structured reporting suite.

Managers can move from the headline result into the locations, products and operational factors behind it, making it easier to understand why performance differs across the business.

What decision-makers can see

From headline sales to what is driving performance.

Commercial performance

Sales, transactions, average basket value, gross margin and target performance are brought together to provide a concise view of how the business is performing.

Managers can explore trends over time, compare individual locations and identify which product categories are contributing most to revenue and margin.

Operational efficiency

Labour and waste are viewed alongside sales rather than in isolation, making it easier to understand whether strong revenue is translating into efficient performance.

Location-level comparisons help reveal where similar sites are producing different outcomes and where further investigation may be worthwhile.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured multi-site data model

Sales, transaction, product, location, staffing, target and waste information are connected through a structured analytical model.

Commercial measures

Sales, transactions, average basket value, gross margin and target performance are calculated consistently across locations and reporting periods.

Operational measures

Labour as a proportion of sales, sales per labour hour and waste measures provide additional context around commercial performance.

Interactive location analysis

Users can move from the organisation-wide result into individual locations, periods and product categories without rebuilding separate reports.

What this demonstrates

Multi-site reporting built around meaningful comparisons.

01

One consistent view

Each location is measured using the same definitions, making comparisons more meaningful and reducing arguments about different versions of the numbers.

02

Commercial and operational data connected

Sales and margin can be considered alongside staffing, waste and product mix rather than being reviewed separately.

03

Analysis without separate reporting packs

Managers can move between locations, products and periods without rebuilding spreadsheets or creating different reports for each site.

04

A reusable management reporting model

The same approach can support businesses with multiple stores, branches, teams, departments or other operational units.
