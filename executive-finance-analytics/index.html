Power BI case study

Executive Financial Performance

An interactive financial reporting suite bringing revenue, profitability, budgets and customer activity together to give decision-makers a clearer view of business performance.

The brief

Move from financial reporting to financial insight.

Financial reporting often involves rebuilding spreadsheets, reconciling different versions of the numbers and manually explaining what has changed.

This Power BI model brings general ledger, budget, customer, site and departmental data into one structured reporting suite.

The report provides an executive view of revenue, profitability and budget performance, alongside deeper analysis of growth, customer concentration and the factors driving revenue.

Dynamic commentary responds to the current filters and explains the selected results in plain language, helping users move from viewing figures to understanding what they mean.

What it shows

Two connected views of financial performance.

Executive Financial Performance

A concise view of revenue, gross profit, operating profit, EBITDA, margins and budget performance, supported by dynamic executive commentary.

Revenue Analysis

A deeper view of revenue growth, budget performance, active customers, average order value, customer concentration and revenue by customer segment.

How it was built

The key pieces.

01

Financial data model

General ledger, sales, budget, customer, site, department and date data connected through a structured analytical model.

02

DAX measures

Revenue, gross profit, operating profit, EBITDA, margins, year-on-year growth and budget variance calculated dynamically.

03

Dynamic commentary

Executive and revenue commentary changes with the filters to interpret performance, growth, budgets and customer dependency.

04

Customer analysis

Customer ranking, active customer counts, average order value, revenue per customer and concentration measures reveal where revenue is coming from.

Business questions

Designed around the decisions leaders need to make.

The report helps users answer questions such as:

  • Are revenue and profitability improving?
  • Is the organisation ahead of or behind budget?
  • Which sites and departments are driving performance?
  • How concentrated is revenue across the customer base?
  • Which customers and sectors generate the most revenue?
  • Are average order values and revenue per customer increasing?
