Power BI case study

Executive Financial Performance

An interactive Power BI reporting suite that brings revenue, profitability, budgets and customer performance together so leaders can see what has changed, understand what is driving it and identify where attention is needed.

The brief

Move from producing the numbers to understanding them.

Financial reporting can consume a significant amount of time before anyone gets to analyse the results. Information may need to be collected from different systems, spreadsheets reconciled and commentary prepared manually each reporting period.

This Power BI model brings general ledger, budget, customer, site and departmental information into one structured reporting suite.

It gives decision-makers an executive view of revenue, profitability and budget performance while allowing them to investigate the customers, sites and business areas driving the result.

Dynamic commentary responds to the current selections and explains the results in plain language, reducing the amount of manual interpretation required.

What decision-makers can see

From the headline result to what is driving it.

Executive performance

Revenue, gross profit, operating profit, EBITDA, margins and budget performance are brought together in a concise management view.

Dynamic commentary highlights significant changes and helps users identify which areas warrant further investigation.

Revenue analysis

Explore revenue growth, budget performance, active customers, average order value, customer concentration and revenue by customer segment.

Users can move from the organisation-wide position into individual sites and departments to understand where performance is coming from.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured financial model

General ledger, sales, budget, customer, site, department and date information are connected through a structured analytical model.

Dynamic financial measures

Revenue, gross profit, operating profit, EBITDA, margins, year-on-year growth and budget variance are calculated dynamically using DAX.

Customer and revenue analysis

Customer ranking, active customer counts, average order value, revenue per customer and concentration measures show where revenue is coming from.

Dynamic commentary

Narrative measures respond to filters and explain performance, growth, budgets and customer dependency in plain language.
