Power BI case study

Gender Pay Gap & Workforce Analytics

An interactive Power BI reporting suite that brings payroll, bonus and workforce data together to explain what is driving the headline gender pay gap — and where attention may be needed.

The brief

Go beyond the headline number.

Gender pay reporting can show that a gap exists. It does not necessarily explain why.

Workforce composition, seniority, departments, locations and bonus participation can all influence the published figures.

This model brings employee, payroll, bonus and organisational data together so decision-makers can move beyond the statutory measures and explore the factors behind them.

Dynamic commentary responds to the current selection and explains the results in plain English.

Live dashboard

Explore the analysis.

Use the filters and visuals to investigate pay gaps, bonus outcomes, workforce composition and representation across the organisation.

What decision-makers can see

A clearer view of pay, reward and representation.

Executive View

Headline measures covering mean and median hourly pay gaps, bonus gaps, workforce headcount and bonus participation.

Dynamic commentary highlights the areas most worth investigating.

Workforce analysis

Explore pay quartiles, grade distribution, department composition, location and seniority to understand how workforce structure affects the overall result.

Interactive filtering allows users to move from organisation-wide outcomes into specific parts of the workforce.

Business questions

Designed around the questions organisations need to answer.

01

What is driving the headline gap?

Explore whether workforce composition, seniority, location or departmental structure is influencing the overall result.

02

Where is representation uneven?

Identify departments, grades and pay quartiles where men or women are under-represented.

03

04

Where should attention be focused?

Use interactive filters and narrative insight to identify the areas that warrant deeper investigation or action.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured data model

Employee, payroll, bonus, location, department and grade data connected through a structured analytical model.

Analytical measures

Mean and median pay gaps, bonus gaps, participation rates, pay quartiles and workforce measures calculated dynamically using DAX.

Dynamic commentary

Narrative measures respond to filters and explain the current results in plain language.

Interactive analysis

Users can move from organisation-wide results into department, location, grade and workforce-level detail.
