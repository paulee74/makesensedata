Power BI case study

Gender Pay Gap & Workforce Analytics

An interactive Power BI reporting suite designed to help organisations understand the factors driving their gender pay gap, bonus outcomes and workforce representation.

The brief

Go beyond the headline number.

Gender pay reporting can show that a gap exists, but the headline result does not necessarily explain what is driving it.

Differences in workforce composition, seniority, departments, locations and bonus participation can all influence the published figures.

This Power BI model combines employee, payroll, bonus and organisational data so users can explore pay, reward and workforce representation in greater depth.

Dynamic commentary interprets the current selection in plain language, helping decision-makers understand the organisational context behind the measures rather than reviewing each figure in isolation.

What it shows

A clearer view of pay, reward and representation.

Executive overview

Headline measures covering mean and median hourly pay gaps, mean and median bonus gaps, workforce headcount and bonus participation.

Dynamic narrative summarises the selected results and highlights the most important areas for further investigation.

Workforce analysis

Explore pay quartiles, grade distribution, department composition, location and seniority to understand how workforce structure affects the overall result.

Interactive filtering allows users to move from organisation-wide outcomes into specific parts of the workforce.

Business questions

Designed around the questions organisations need to answer.

01

What is driving the headline gap?

Explore whether workforce composition, seniority, location or departmental structure is influencing the overall result.

02

Where is representation uneven?

Identify departments, grades and pay quartiles where men or women are under-represented.

03

Are bonus outcomes consistent?

Compare bonus participation and bonus values to understand how reward is distributed across the workforce.

04

Where should attention be focused?

Use interactive filters and narrative insight to identify the areas that warrant deeper investigation or action.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured data model

Employee, payroll, bonus, location, department and grade data connected through a structured analytical model.

Analytical measures

Mean and median pay gaps, bonus gaps, participation rates, pay quartiles and workforce measures calculated dynamically using DAX.

Dynamic commentary

Narrative measures respond to filters and explain the current results in plain language.

Interactive analysis

Users can move from organisation-wide results into department, location, grade and workforce-level detail.

Techniques demonstrated

More than a statutory report.

01

Executive KPIs

Clear headline measures designed to give senior users an immediate view of pay and reward outcomes.

02

Pay quartile analysis

Workforce distribution is analysed across four pay bands to show how representation changes through the organisation.

03

Dynamic narrative

Executive commentary changes with the filters and explains the selected results without requiring users to interpret every visual independently.

04

Decision-focused design

Each visual supports a practical business question rather than simply displaying available workforce data.
