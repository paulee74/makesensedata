Power BI case study

Gender Pay Gap & Workforce Analytics

An interactive reporting suite bringing payroll, bonus and workforce data together to show not just the headline gender pay gap, but the organisational context behind it.

The brief

Go beyond the headline number.

Gender pay reporting can show that a gap exists, but it does not necessarily explain what is driving it.

This Power BI model combines employee, payroll, bonus and organisational data so users can explore pay, reward and workforce representation by department, location and seniority.

Dynamic narrative measures interpret the current selection in plain language, helping turn the metrics into something decision-makers can use.

Live dashboard

Explore the analysis.

Use the filters and visuals to investigate pay gaps, bonus outcomes, representation and workforce composition.

Demonstration dashboard using synthetic data. No real employee, organisation or client information is shown.

How it was built

The key pieces.

01

Data model

Employee, payroll, bonus, location, department and grade data connected in a structured analytical model.

02

DAX measures

Mean and median pay gaps, bonus gaps, participation, pay quartiles and workforce metrics calculated dynamically.

03

Dynamic narrative

Executive commentary changes with the filters to explain the selected results in plain language.

04

Interactive analysis

Users can move from organisation-wide results into department, location and workforce-level detail.
