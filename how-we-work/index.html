How we work

Practical reporting that grows with your organisation.

Most organisations do not need a full-time data team. They need reliable reporting, someone who understands their business and support when things change.

The model

A reporting service, not a dashboard that gets left behind.

Make Sense Data combines an initial reporting solution with ongoing monthly support.

The starting point is usually one of a growing range of proven reporting frameworks, adapted around your organisation, your data and the decisions your teams need to make.

Once the reporting is live, a monthly support arrangement helps keep it refreshed, maintained and useful as requirements change.

Additional development can be added when you need something beyond the original scope, giving you flexibility without turning every small request into a completely new project.

Step one

Start with the business question.

Good reporting begins by understanding what people are trying to achieve, not by deciding which chart to build.

Before development begins, I want to understand:

  • Which decisions are difficult today?
  • What reporting already exists?
  • Where does the data come from?
  • Which processes take too long?
  • Which figures are difficult to trust?
  • What should someone be able to understand at a glance?

Sometimes the answer is a dashboard. Sometimes it is a better process, clearer definitions or a simpler way of bringing information together.

Initial setup

Launch the first reporting solution.

Once the scope is agreed, the chosen reporting framework is configured around your organisation.

Depending on the project, the initial build may include:

  • Connecting and preparing your data
  • Creating the reporting model
  • Agreeing KPI definitions
  • Building measures and calculations
  • Tailoring dashboards and commentary
  • Testing and reconciling the figures
  • Publishing the report
  • Training and handover

The exact scope is agreed before development begins, so both sides are clear about what the initial solution will include.

Top-up development

Add development time when you need more.

Additional development hours can be purchased when you need work beyond the monthly support allowance or the original project scope.

This gives you a flexible way to commission enhancements without moving immediately into a large new project.

Suitable uses might include:

  • Adding a new report page
  • Building additional measures
  • Extending commentary
  • Creating a new data connection
  • Adding automation
  • Developing a new analytical feature

Where a request is substantial, it will be quoted as a separate project rather than drawn down informally.

Why this approach

Reporting should evolve with the organisation.

Traditional consultancy often ends when the dashboard is handed over.

The organisation then changes, source files move, new questions emerge and the reporting gradually becomes less useful.

This model combines a defined initial build with ongoing support, allowing the reporting to remain relevant without every small adjustment becoming a separate procurement exercise.

It also gives organisations access to business and technical expertise without the cost of employing a full-time reporting resource.
