Power BI case study

Emergency Department Analytics

An interactive reporting suite designed to help operational and clinical leaders understand Emergency Department demand, patient flow, waiting times and performance.

The brief

Turn complex patient-flow data into clear operational insight.

Emergency Departments generate large volumes of operational and clinical information, but individual measures do not always show where pressure is building or what is affecting patient flow.

This Power BI model brings attendance, triage, clinician, arrival, outcome and waiting-time data together in a structured analytical model.

The report provides an executive overview of demand and performance, alongside a deeper operational view of triage, clinical workload, admission rates and length of stay.

Dynamic commentary interprets the current selection in plain language, helping users identify the main operational pressures rather than simply reviewing individual measures.

What it shows

Two connected views of Emergency Department performance.

Executive Dashboard

A high-level view of attendances, four-hour performance, waiting time to clinician, length of stay, admission rate, patients leaving before being seen and ambulance arrivals.

A demand heatmap shows average patient arrivals by hour and day, while monthly trends and presenting-complaint analysis help explain the pattern of activity.

Operational Performance & Patient Flow

A detailed operational view focused on patients seen, admissions, transfers, discharges, triage time and clinician-level workload.

The page also compares triage waiting times, admission rates by age group, length of stay by complaint and operational performance by doctor.

Business questions

Designed around the decisions operational teams need to make.

01

When does demand peak?

Identify recurring arrival patterns by hour, day and month to support staffing and capacity planning.

02

Where are delays occurring?

Compare waiting time, triage performance and length of stay to understand where patient flow is slowing.

03

Which presentations create pressure?

Explore presenting complaints, acuity, admission rates and patient outcomes to identify the most resource-intensive demand.

04

How consistent is performance?

Compare operational measures across clinicians and patient groups while retaining the wider context of case mix and demand.

How it was built

The key pieces.

Structured data model

Attendance, clinician, triage, complaint and outcome data connected through a Power BI analytical model.

Operational measures

Measures covering attendances, admissions, waiting times, length of stay, four-hour performance, ambulance arrivals and patients leaving before being seen.

Dynamic narrative

Commentary measures respond to the current filters and summarise demand, patient flow and operational pressure in plain language.

Interactive analysis

Users can move from executive performance into patient flow, clinician workload, age profiles, complaint mix and triage performance.
