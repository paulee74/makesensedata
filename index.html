Why Make Sense Data

Data should reduce uncertainty, not create more of it.

I’ve worked with organisations that are overwhelmed by the amount of data they collect, unsure what to do with it, or simply don’t trust the information in front of them.

I’ve seen reporting that takes days to produce, relying on manual, non-repeatable processes where the potential for human error is always present.

I’ve seen different departments producing different reports from the same data, leaving people trying to work out which version of the truth they should believe.

People shouldn’t have to wonder whether they can trust the figures they’re using to make important decisions.

That’s why Make Sense Data exists.

Our role is to simplify reporting, reduce manual effort and help organisations trust the information they’re using to make decisions.
