Why Make Sense Data

Data should reduce uncertainty, not create more of it.

I’ve worked with organisations where the problem isn’t a lack of data. It’s knowing what to do with it.

The challenge is turning all that information into something people can actually use.

Too much data

Information is collected across different systems, spreadsheets and reports, but getting to the answer you actually need can be surprisingly difficult.

Too much manual reporting

I’ve seen reporting that takes days to produce, relying on repetitive processes where there is always the potential for human error.

Too many versions of the truth

Different departments produce different reports from the same data, leaving people trying to work out which numbers they should believe.

People shouldn’t have to wonder whether they can trust the figures they’re using to make important decisions.

That’s why Make Sense Data exists.

I build practical reporting and data solutions around the questions an organisation actually needs to answer.
