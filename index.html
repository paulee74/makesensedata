The point Data does not have to be complicated to be valuable.

I focus on specific reporting and data problems where experience, common sense and practical delivery matter more than a large consultancy team.

Why businesses get in touch

Usually, something has stopped working.

Businesses rarely start by asking for a dashboard. They usually have a reporting problem, a regulatory deadline or a process that has become too difficult to manage.

Reporting has not kept up with growth

The spreadsheets and manual processes that worked for a smaller business are now taking too long and no longer give managers the information they need.

Regulatory reporting is creating pressure

A deadline is approaching, a return is difficult to produce or a regulator has identified weaknesses that need to be resolved accurately and quickly.

Staff are rebuilding the same reports

Skilled employees are spending too much time copying, cleansing and combining information instead of using it to investigate problems and make decisions.

The answer is not always more technology. Sometimes it is simply a better way of using what is already there.

That is where I can help

I bring experience across business operations, finance, healthcare, regulation, reporting, data quality and migration, allowing me to get up to speed quickly and focus on practical results.

How I work

Listen first. Build something useful. Refine it together.

Every project starts by sitting with the people who understand the business and listening to what is causing difficulty.

I then build an initial version, either from a defined reporting framework or from a clean starting point. That first version gives everyone something tangible to work with.

From there, we hone it together until it answers the questions the business genuinely needs answered.

The aim is not simply to deliver a report. It is to create something people continue to use and support it as requirements change.

What success looks like

A report is only useful if people continue to use it.

I do not measure a successful project by whether a dashboard was delivered. I measure it by what changes for the people using it.

01

People get time back

Staff spend less time rebuilding reports and more time using information to do useful work.

02

Decisions become easier

Users can get to the important information without wading through rows of data or several versions of the same report.

03

Reporting is more reliable

Information is prepared consistently, checked properly and available when it is needed.

04

The work continues to provide value

The best result is hearing that something built months or years ago is still helping the business today.
